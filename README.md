# Día de Muertos · ORB

Open `index.html`. The sphere, six-direction compass, point reading, media, and player stay together in the ORB. All 52 credited real photographs and 13 original-publisher videos belong to mapped points. There is no separate photo gallery or video website.

## Listening

**Heart (English)** and **Dora (Spanish)** are available for both point readings and **ORB Feel & Experience — Guided Audio Journey**. Select the mode, then audio language. Text follows the selected recording when it plays. Interface language is separately selectable.

The default pace is **0.94×**, 6% slower than the supplied recordings, with pitch preserved. Pace is adjustable. At this pace the complete Feel & Experience journey is **25:16 in English** and **24:40 in Spanish**. Its own continuous bilingual script traverses all 37 original points, retaining sources and qualifications. The browse readings remain separate and unchanged.

**Start from the top** resets to the opening. **Listen from this point** starts the selected passage. **Resume saved place** displays and returns to the parked point and position for that mode and language. Switching modes or languages pauses old audio and keeps the point, starting the new matching passage at its beginning. Browsing pauses the course and preserves its listening place. Auto-next follows all 37 points and ends clearly. Each language and mode has its own saved progress. Save/Restore also supports portable JSON files when browser storage is unavailable.

The full guided scripts are `feel-en.md` and `feel-es.md`; `feel.json` holds source-point mappings. ORB Learn remains an optional script-authoring continuation action; it is not advertised as a completed separate course recording.

## Media and evidence

Photo captions, photographers, source pages, licenses, and file hashes are preserved in `photos.json`. Each photo retains its own Creative Commons license. No blanket relicensing is claimed. Photos are resized and compressed; display crops vary. No AI-generated photographs are used.

Video titles, publishers, and links were verified. Videos have not all been watched end to end. Embedded availability can vary by publisher, device, region, and connection; every video has a direct YouTube fallback. The parade embed reported unavailable in the local preview. The ORB language switch does not dub publisher videos.

English and Spanish scripts were authored directly by the primary assistant. Guided scenes are explicitly imagined and do not invent interviews or testimony. Heart now uses explicit Spanish phonemes for Día de Muertos (including the variant with los) inside English sentences. Only the ten affected English passages were regenerated; original recordings are preserved in production backups. Local Kokoro v1.0 ONNX supplied the recordings at synthesis speed 0.9. All 148 browse and guided recordings have been decoded and checked against their exact scripts and voice metadata. Representative browser playback is tested; a full human listening review has not been performed.
