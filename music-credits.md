# Día de Muertos · Soundscape / Paisaje sonoro

The optional background combines a locally generated instrumental with real field recordings from Mexico. It accompanies both English and Spanish narration. It is an imagined sound design for this ORB, not a recording of a particular Día de Muertos ceremony or parade.

El fondo opcional combina música instrumental generada localmente con grabaciones reales realizadas en México. Acompaña la narración en inglés y español. Es un diseño sonoro imaginado para este ORB; no es la grabación de una ceremonia o un desfile específico del Día de Muertos.

- **Instrumental / Música:** produced for ORB Journeys using local ACE-Step 1.5, seed 26090937. A Mexican and Latin American acoustic ambient arrangement, prompted for nylon-string guitar, marimba and warm sustained harmonies. Instrumental stem isolated with Demucs, edited, equalized and looped. This is AI-generated accompaniment, not an archival or traditional ceremonial performance.
- **Oaxaca street marching band / Banda en las calles de Oaxaca:** “Brass band with drums - part 4” by **pnwheeler**, recorded around Oaxaca's Zócalo and cathedral on **July 29, 2023**. [Original recording](https://freesound.org/people/pnwheeler/sounds/831651/). Published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Public audio preview excerpted, filtered, compressed, looped and mixed at a distant level.
- **Mexican market / Mercado mexicano:** “Market fair ambience with crowd hubbub far and cars passing” by **Félix Blume**, recorded in Mexico in **December 2009**, reference MX091212-064. [Original recording](https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/705302/). Published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Public audio preview excerpted, filtered, compressed, looped and mixed quietly.

The field recordings are from different places and occasions. Their inclusion does not imply the recordists or musicians endorse this ORB. Original narration files and matching transcripts remain unchanged. Website music has its own volume and on/off control and follows narration playback.

Las grabaciones de campo provienen de distintos lugares y ocasiones. Su inclusión no implica que sus autores o músicos respalden este ORB. Los archivos originales de narración y sus transcripciones permanecen intactos. En el ORB puedes ajustar o desactivar el fondo; este sigue la reproducción de la narración.
